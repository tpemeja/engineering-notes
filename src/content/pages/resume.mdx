## Summary

Backend and platform engineer with 5 years building Python and FastAPI APIs and AWS-based infrastructure for edge and cloud systems in IoT and cybersecurity. Sole maintainer of the device-management API running on an edge AI fleet, and designer of its pluggable remote-access layer. Previously cut new-customer deployment from 3-4 days to 2 hours at an XDR security vendor, and led APAC incident triage after being selected to open its Tokyo office.
