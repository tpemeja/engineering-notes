## Building

Right now my time is split between two things.

[Kataribe](/projects/kataribe/) is a hackathon prototype due on
2026-10-18: a voice AI that talks with Japanese seniors for a few minutes
a day and turns their memories into a life story their family can read,
hear and ask more about. It runs on the Gemini Live API, and the hard part
is making sure the AI never puts words in their mouth.

The rest goes into a set of tools around my wife's Instagram cooking
account. The [Instagram Agent](/projects/instagram-agent/) is a
serverless AWS backend that takes in Meta webhooks, polls post insights,
and drafts replies to comments and DMs with an LLM, though a person still
confirms anything before it's sent. On top of it sit a
[web dashboard](/projects/instagram-dashboard/) and an
[always-on tablet](/projects/instagram-insight/), and
[VideoEdit](/projects/videoedit/) writes the bilingual captions for her
videos.

Outside those two, [FastSpec](/projects/fastspec/), my app for writing,
versioning and linting OpenAPI specs, is now open source. You can
self-host it from one Docker image, and its MCP server lets AI agents read
and edit specs.

[Belote AI](/projects/belote-ai/) has grown well past getting the game
into OpenSpiel. My seeding fix landed upstream as
[open_spiel#1584](https://github.com/google-deepmind/open_spiel/pull/1584),
and the agents now run from hand-written rules to Monte Carlo search to
networks that match that search at 148x the speed. The next step is the
language model: trying larger models, and letting the model propose a few
cards for Monte Carlo to check instead of picking alone.
